momottakr is provided by momottakr ("we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Information Stored on Your Device

momottakr stores the information you enter, including transaction amounts, income and expense categories, dates, notes, and budget settings, locally on your device. This information is used to display your ledger, calculate totals, generate charts, and track your budget.

We do not operate a server that receives your financial records. No account or registration is required.

Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

The app does not include advertising, third-party analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking. We do not sell or rent your financial records or use them for targeted advertising.

Apple may separately process information related to App Store downloads, device diagnostics, or system services under its own policies and your device settings.

Exports and Device Backups

You can export your transactions as a CSV file. The exported file contains your transaction details, including any notes you entered.

You choose where to save the file. If you select iCloud Drive or another external storage provider, that provider handles the exported file under its own terms and privacy policy.

Depending on your device settings, app data may also be included in backups managed by Apple or your computer. momottakr does not provide its own cloud synchronization service.

Retention and Deletion

Your records remain in the app until you delete them or delete the app from your device. Offloading the app may retain its documents and data.

Deleting a transaction or the app does not automatically remove previously exported files or copies in device backups. You can manage those copies through the relevant storage provider or backup settings.

Because we do not receive your local financial records, we cannot access, recover, or delete them remotely.

Support Communications

If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address and message. We use that information to respond to your request and resolve support issues. Our email service provider may process these communications.

We retain support communications only as reasonably necessary to address your request and meet applicable legal obligations. Please avoid sending sensitive financial information unless it is necessary for your request.

Security

The app uses local storage within the iOS app sandbox. Protect your device with a passcode and keep your operating system updated. Exported CSV files are not password-protected by momottakr.

Policy Updates

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the app or its information practices. The revised policy will include an updated effective date.

Contact

For questions about momottakr, this Privacy Policy, or these Terms of Use, please contact us.
